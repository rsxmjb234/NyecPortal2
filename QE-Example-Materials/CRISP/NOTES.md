# CRISP (Maryland) — design reference only

- Official site: https://www.crisphealth.org
- **Not a New York SHIN-NY QE.** CRISP is the Chesapeake Regional Information
  System for our Patients (Maryland's statewide HIE). Included here only as a
  look/feel and capability reference.
- Support line referenced on site: 877-952-7477.

## Positioning / messaging (paraphrased from crisphealth.org)
- Connects health systems, providers, and other healthcare entities so
  comprehensive, up-to-date data is available for clinical decisions.
- Aggregates data from many sources (hospitals, primary care, specialty) into a
  **unified health record** to streamline care coordination and reduce
  duplicate services.
- Resource Library of user guides, fact sheets, and videos; dedicated account
  managers guide onboarding.

## Brand / look & feel
- Teal/green primary with navy; clean, card-based, "actionable data" layout —
  part of the inspiration for the demo's card dashboard.
