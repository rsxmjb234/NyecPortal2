# Hixny (Capital Region)

- Official site: https://hixny.org
- Region: Capital Region of New York (and into Vermont). SHIN-NY Qualified Entity.
- ~25+ years operating; emphasis on point-of-care workflow integration.

## Positioning / messaging (paraphrased from hixny.org)
- Tagline theme: shared health information you can **act on**; connects the
  healthcare community to the SHIN-NY to improve care and streamline workflows.
- Pillars they stress: **direct workflow integration** (launches inside EHR
  point-of-care workflows, "one click"), strong data quality + security,
  long-term community commitment, user-led product development, and local
  responsive support.
- They contrast themselves with peers by **parsing CCD data and surfacing
  insights** rather than just showing CCDs as PDFs.

## Brand / look & feel
- Lowercase "hixny" wordmark; teal/blue primary with a warm orange accent.
- Clean, modern, workflow-first feel — informed the "Hixny style" (SMART on
  FHIR-like card dashboard) view in the demo.
